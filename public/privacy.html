Chillax
Your data, kept quiet

Privacy

Chillax is designed to work without an account or a personal-data backend. Most information stays in your browser, on your device.

The short version: Chillax does not sell your information. Traffic details are sent to Google only when you use the Traffic tool, and the resulting route is not stored by Chillax.

Information saved on your device

Chillax uses browser storage to remember app preferences. If you use Traffic, this can include your home destination, preferred arrival time, and arrival cushion. These preferences remain on the device and browser where you entered them; Chillax does not upload them to its own server.

You can remove saved information with Chillax’s reset control or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Traffic and precise location

Traffic is optional. When you ask for a route, Chillax may request your device’s precise location, or use a starting point that you enter. It sends the starting point, your destination, and route timing information directly from your browser to Google Maps Platform so Google can calculate and display the route.

Chillax keeps the current route result only in temporary app memory. It does not persist your precise location, route response, travel time, or map route after the page is reloaded. Google processes these requests under its own Privacy Policy and Maps Platform Terms.

Weather and photography

When you use Weather, your browser may send a place name or approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo to retrieve geocoding and forecast information. Weather photography is served from the Chillax site and is sourced from Unsplash with attribution. Open-Meteo and the hosting provider receive ordinary technical request information and handle it under their own policies.

No accounts, advertising profile, or sale of data

Chillax does not require an account, maintain a user-profile database, or sell personal information. This standard Chillax build does not use information from Traffic or Weather to create an advertising profile.

Browser and hosting information

Like most websites, the hosting provider and third-party services may receive limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type, request time, and requested URL for security and delivery. The person or organization operating a particular Chillax deployment controls its hosting configuration and is responsible for describing any additional analytics or services they add.

Changes and questions

This notice may change as Chillax changes. The latest version posted with a deployment applies to that deployment. For questions, contact the person or organization that provided or operates the Chillax site you are using.
