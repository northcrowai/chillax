# Third-party audio

Chillax includes a small, curated pack of freely reusable recordings and music. The files are served from the same origin as the app and loaded only when selected. They are not part of the initial PWA precache.

All included recordings are either dedicated to the public domain with CC0 1.0 or explicitly released into the public domain. Attribution is retained here and in the in-app settings even when the license does not require it.
